How it is built

Grid
64 × 64 units, corner radius 14 (22 %).
Trace
Starts flat on the ground line (y 46), climbs in three steps to the end point (y 17). The steps read as a motor climb followed by lift.
Margins
12 units left, 13 right, 12 top and bottom. The trace is visually centred, not mathematically.
Line
5 units, round ends and corners. The end dot is 10 units wide and marks “now”.
Colour
Teal #0d6a83, the app's accent. White trace. The same icon works on light and dark.

Sizes

At 24 px and below, the three steps blur into noise. The small version has two steps and a thicker line. The app switches automatically: favicons use the small version, everything else the full one.

Versions

Colour

Default everywhere.

One colour

For print, stickers, engraving. Trace is cut out.

Trace only

Inside text or small UI spots, e.g. a loading state.

App icon

Full-bleed square for phone home screens. Phones cut it to a rounded square or a circle; the trace stays inside the safe zone.

Rules

Clear space
Keep free space around the logo at least as wide as the end dot (⅙ of the icon).
Minimum size
16 px for the icon (small version), 80 px wide for icon plus name.
Name
Always “TLMscope”: “TLM” in capitals and bold, “scope” in lower case, no space. Typeface Barlow Semi Condensed.
Don't
Stretch, rotate, recolour the trace, or put the colour icon on busy photos without its square.
