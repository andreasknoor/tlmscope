tlmscope · naming and logo

How the name could look

“tlmscope” in lower case reads like a file name. Below are two routes: a better spelling with no logo, or a small logo next to the name. Every option is shown on light and dark, as a browser-tab icon and in the app's top bar.

Route 1 · Spelling only

Cheapest to maintain: no icon to design or scale. The browser tab then needs a letter icon.

Route 2 · Logo plus name

Each logo is paired with spelling A. The icon also works alone as the browser-tab and home-screen icon.

My recommendation

Logo A (climb trace) with the spelling TLMscope. It says what the app does: it shows a flight as a curve. It stays readable at 16 px, and its single colour fits the app's existing accent. Logo D is the most “model flying”, but a glider shape is harder to read at small sizes and ties the app to gliders. Powered models and helicopters write the same logs.

The logos are first sketches to choose a direction. Once you pick one, I would refine the shapes and spacing and add the icon files the app needs.
